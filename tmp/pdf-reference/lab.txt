 Clinical Significance
 C-Reeactive Protein (CRP) is an acute phase protein synthesized in the liver.Normally, it is present only in trace amounts in serum, but it can
increases much as 1,000-fold in response to injury or infection. The clinical measurment of CRP in serum therefore appears  to be a valuable
screening test for organic disease and a sensitive index of disease activity in inflammatory, infective and ischemic conditios. During tissue necrosis
and inflammation resulting from microbial infections, the CRP concentration can ise to 300 mg/L in 12-24 hours.
